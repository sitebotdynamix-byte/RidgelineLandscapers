Local roots, long views

Marnie Callahan started Ridgeline in 2009 with one used mower and a trailer borrowed from her uncle. Today a crew of eleven keeps more than 300 Laramie-area properties looking sharp.

We plant what survives here, build for the frost, and tell you honestly when a lawn should become a meadow. Fully insured, with a one-year guarantee on every planting.

Laramie sits at 7,200 feet, where the wind rarely rests and a late frost can arrive in June. That is why we favor deep-rooted natives, mulch that stays put, and irrigation that sips instead of soaks. The result is a yard that looks good in August and still looks like itself in March.

Common questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every job starts with a free walk-through and a written estimate. Call or email to set up a time.

When is the best time to plant in Laramie?

Late May through June suits most perennials. Early September is best for trees and shrubs, after the worst of the wind and before hard frost.

Do you work year-round?

Landscaping and lawn care run April through October. Snow removal runs November through April.

How do I pay?

We accept card, check or bank transfer. Invoices are due within 14 days of completion.
